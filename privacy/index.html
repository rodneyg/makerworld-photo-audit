Photo Audit for MakerWorld

Privacy policy

The extension author does not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal data. The extension has no tracking, analytics, advertising, or AI services.

What the extension reads

When you start a scan, the extension reads public model links on the selected MakerWorld profile. When the Models page links to another results page, the extension can read it with a public request. It sends each model ID directly to Bambu Lab’s public design API to retrieve photo flags, counts, and any available download or point counts. These requests omit credentials. Bambu Lab handles those requests under its own privacy policy.

What stays in your browser

Audit results are processed in Chrome. The extension caches recent public design metadata in Chrome and refreshes cached entries after 15 minutes when requested again. Uninstalling the extension removes its cache. CSV files are saved to your device only when you choose Export CSV.
