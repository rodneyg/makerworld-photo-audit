# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name

Photo Audit for MakerWorld

## Short description

Check MakerWorld model cover flags and real-life photo counts. Find cover risks, flag edits that may be blocked, and export CSV.

## Long description

Open the Models tab of a public MakerWorld profile and scan its model photos. See which covers MakerWorld explicitly flags as unmarked, how many gallery photos MakerWorld marks as real-life, and which cover edits may be blocked by the three-photo check.

- See edit-blocked models and cover risks first, with model links and public download or point counts where available.
- Export your results as a CSV.
- Open the audit in a tab to keep it handy while paging through models.

The audit uses MakerWorld's public metadata. Review flagged models yourself to confirm that the cover shows the complete, finished print. For older models, review the cover and check the gallery before changing the cover.

Free, read-only, and independent of MakerWorld and Bambu Lab. No AI, tracking, or account access.
